Two paths, one question

How does intelligence compute? I come at it from both sides: studying it in the brain, and building it in machines.

For academia

Neuroscience & NeuroAI research

I'm a Master's student in Neuroscience (Machine Learning specialization) at Western University in the Mohsenzadeh Lab, a Vector Institute–affiliated lab, supervised by Dr. Yalda Mohsenzadeh and Dr. Emma Duerden. I study visual processing with simultaneous EEG–fNIRS, combining the timing of electrical activity with the slower hemodynamic response to see how the brain represents and remembers what we see.

Before Western, I completed an MSc in AI & Robotics at the University of Tehran with Dr. Mohammadreza Abolghasemi Dehaqani. My thesis used an RSVP paradigm and 128-channel EEG to separate repetition and expectation effects in the visual mismatch negativity, within the predictive-coding framework.

For industry

Applied & trustworthy AI

I'm an ML researcher with a computer-engineering foundation and 4+ years of building end-to-end pipelines on high-dimensional, noisy real-world data. My focus is making model outputs trustworthy, through interpretability, uncertainty quantification and calibration, across deep networks in PyTorch and LLM-based systems.

Recently I've been building with foundation models: measuring and fixing overconfidence in vision-language models, and shipping a multimodal, guideline-grounded LLM triage pipeline at Hack Western 12. I'm looking for Applied AI Research Scientist and Data Scientist roles in Toronto / GTA.
